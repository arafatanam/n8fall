Scripts stories
and publishes them

n8fall is a personal automation script that turns a subject into a narrated short video, then publishes it to a single YouTube channel. Built and run by one developer, for one channel.

01

Writes the script

Given a subject, a language model drafts a short narration in a consistent tone.

02

Builds the video

The script is turned into narration, subtitles, and stock footage, then rendered into a finished video.

03

Publishes to YouTube

The finished video is uploaded directly to the developer's own channel, either right away or scheduled for later.

About access

n8fall isn't a public service. It's a single-user script, run by its developer, that authorizes itself to upload videos to the developer's own YouTube channel. It doesn't accept logins from other people or act on anyone else's account.
